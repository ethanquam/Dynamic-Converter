Accepted input formats
  • Meters / feet / inches: plain decimals (3.048, 10.5)
  • Feet-inches: 12'-6 1/2", 12-6 1/2, 12 ft 6 1/2 in
  • Inches only: 6 1/2" or 78.5 in
  • Feet only: 10' or 10 ft
  • Calculator (Imperial): tap digits, ft, &, ⁄, and in (e.g. 4'6&1/2")
  • Calculator (Metric): enter decimal meters (e.g. 1.5 or 0.3048)
  • Calculator (Standard): plain decimal numbers — no units, no converter export
